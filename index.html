College Financial Health Explorer

How can you figure out how a college is doing financially?

It can be tough for students and families to find answers. The Hechinger Report created this free tool to help at a critical moment.

As financial pressures mount, colleges are also facing threats from the Trump administration to international student enrollment, graduate student borrowing and research funding.

Meanwhile, small colleges tend to publicize limited financial information. Private nonprofits are exempt from public records laws.
