College Financial Health Tracker

New to this tool? Here's a guide.

How can you figure out how healthy a college’s finances are?

It can be tough for students and families to find answers. The Hechinger Report created this free tool to help.

As financial pressures mount, colleges are also facing threats to international student enrollment, graduate student borrowing and research funding.

Meanwhile, small colleges tend to publicize limited financial information. Private nonprofits are exempt from public records laws.

In the search bar, look up a four-year college or university for key financial and enrollment trends over the past decade.
